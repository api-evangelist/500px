Eline Deckers works in hard light and is not afraid of the dark. The Antwerp-based photographer shoots under the name Studio EDX, and she makes black-and-white street photography with the contrast turned all the way up. A figure in a wide-brimmed hat is reduced to a silhouette in a black alley.
