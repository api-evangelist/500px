Fog changes everything it touches. Familiar streets become mysterious, busy landscapes turn quiet, and cluttered backgrounds dissolve into soft grey. Fog photography is one of the most rewarding forms of weather photography because the conditions do so much of the compositional work for you, reducing a scene to shape, tone, and mood.
